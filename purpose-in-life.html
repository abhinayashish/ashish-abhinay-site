What Is The Purpose Of Having Purpose In Life?

Could it be true that Mahatma Gandhi was trying to unalive himself every time he organised a hunger strike?

He picks an issue and makes demands that no self-respecting colonising power would agree to. Everything is carefully planned. Deliberate even.

Then he waits, satiating his appetite with the sweetness of approaching death. He is reaching out his hand. People think he is yearning for food (they shake their heads in grief), but Gandhi doesn't care. He can see the light at the end of the tunnel. He is there. And soon will become has been—

A cheer breaks out of the crowd. The British have conceded to his demands.

Gandhi wants to fume, 'such cowards,' but he can't. He is bereft of energy. So he smiles at his own fate. Everyone smiles with him. He's given a roti to munch on, but the curry has no salt in it.

Why, he asks?

Tax, they shout back.

He takes in a deep breath. 'On to the next thing,' he says to himself. This time, they'd better not give in.

What? You never thought about this? Not even once. Okay, lets move on then.

Now that i have your attention lets dig in.

Purpose. Meaning. Direction… stability?

What is the point of life?

First of all, why is it a point? Why not a rectangle? A circle? Or a trapezium… that's the extent of my knowledge of shapes.

You know more? Good for you.

Before we even address the question, I think it's better to tackle the purposes humans assign themselves. Let's do it one by one.

AMBITION

To most, being ambitious is a very desirable quality. I personally have beef with ambition. I think human ambition had done more bad than it has done good. Don't believe me? Let's take examples.

Genghis Khan was ambitious. Some would argue his ambition is the most ambition has ever ambitioned. The result: 40 million killed. The only positive, all the decaying bodies probably added a lot of nutrition to the top soil. Yes, I can give him that. The most a person had ever done for our nature and climate change. Kudos. Cheers.

In the present we have Dolund trumpet. The most ambitious man of our times. This man sitting all the way across the world, doing what he pleases, has someone made me prowl through the city to find an LPG gas cylinder. His ambition has almost become the death of me by dehydration. And i don't even make the top 10,000 people grievously hurt by dolund's actions.

If all the good human ambition had done adds up to the size of an asteroid, then the bad would have to a star. My choice is Alpha Centuri. How can something so irreversibly ugly be the purpose of our entire species?

FAITH

Is it God? Our purpose?

This greater being who is capable of no wrongdoing. Who, in spite of being so great and all encompassing, couldn't come up with a better hobby than people watching? What is he/she even doing nowadays since most of us have refrained from kicking or killing each other.

You are bored out of your mind, scrolling reels, and apparently, God is watching you watch the reels. Judging you based on your feed. Why aren't we allowed to judge him for not doing something better with his existence? Does he not realise what a god's gift it is to live? Why is he wasting it? And oh, he supposedly hates questions. Loves complete surrender.

Faith requires obedience and acceptance, to shut down. Most people find it appealing. For it is simple, vapid, easy. The answer is always the same no matter the question. Which makes questions irrelevant. But being curious is our species foundation.

Faith might make more sense if it were reserved only for simpler creatures like dogs, or boars, or bears, or cats. Maybe not cats. Cats don't take to obedience well. Besides, I think they have gods of their own, nine lives and all…

LOVE

Ah, love. This magical, elusive thing, this sparkle of joy dangling off of a tree, mostly unreachable. If and when you do manage to grab it- it supposedly loses its shine. I don't know. All this is hearsay. I never got to experience it myself.

For me it's less elusive and more hidden behind a door. Locked. Never revealing itself. So, for that offence alone, I refuse to talk about love.

Yeah… I just had a conversation with my mom and realised love also encompasses family and friends. Love is so selfless, so noble.

Love is selfless. so you give up your ways, maybe career, maybe freewill… yes so very selfless. Of you. It still makes your partner selfish for making you do them. Oh they sacrifice for me too. its kinda like someone pushes you into a sewer and then they join you. it stinks. but you have each other to close your noses. problem solved. woo hoo.

Everyone has friends and family. So, everyone gets to experience love. So it gets to be a purpose because of how approachable it is.

Sadly, humans die. I am sorry if you haven't noticed until now, and if my revelation shook you. Take a deep breath.

How can the purpose of your life die? Shouldn't it be with you, guiding you till the very end? die along with you?

It's nice this love, but sadly, we humans can't love ourselves enough, so we latch onto other humans.

REPRODUCTION

I am not talking about the act itself but the result of it. Though I have heard good things about the process, good for the senses apparently. Yeah… right. ok.

Kids. The answer, the result is kids. To be fair, this is done by every species on planet earth. And that's precisely why we can't have the same purpose.

How embarrassing to have the same purpose in life as cockroaches, camels, or octopuses.

We, with our splendid self- conscious and supreme intelligence, should probably breed so that there could be more of us. How is this beneficial to me as an individual? My self-conscious screams at me.

It's not that our brilliance has come up with a more sophisticated reason behind reproduction. The best we had come up with is, "Who is going to take care of me when I grow older? No, not the billion other humans around me. They are all selfish.

No, I am going to create another human for this. No, there is nothing self-regarding about this.

if not that, then a myriad other selfish reasons. The act of such purity, giving birth, is reduced to human self-obsession. Mortality. To continue legacy. Act all entitled the rest of their lives as if the kids asked to be given birth. God's gift, sure, but actually, I gave birth to you. Now go do what I say.

DEATH

I just read a book where a character says, "destination is grave. People who hurry in life tend to get to the destination quicker than others who take their time."

Only few believe in death as a purpose for a righteous cause and they are martyrs. And to somehow believe that death could mean more than life is nothing but vanity.

Many people who take their lives may have lived with unease as their constant companion.

Death aids to remove the unease, but does the mere removal of it mean that now you are at ease or comfortable? Does eliminating un from uncomfortable make one comfortable? No. It leaves us in an unknown state, a state with no name- to not be. To not feel. where the dead live. How could the very essence of not being or doing be a purpose?

These are, as far as I know, broadly the purposes humans latch onto.

Now, let's circle back to the question, 'What's the purpose of having a purpose in life?'

How come we equate having one in life to peace and happiness. whats so wrong with uncertainty. Why does it make us restless?

To try to answer that, I must recite an anecdote from the book Martyr by Kaveh Akbar. I mean, I can't physically recite it to you, so maybe read it out loud yourself. It's an old school, discarded Muslim hadith.

"Satan hears about Adam, the first man, this new creation, who is apparently God's favourite creation. Satan steps into his mouth, disappears entirely inside him, and passes through all his gut and intestines and comes out of Adam's anus. When he is out, Satan is shaking with laughter. He rolls around, unable to control his laugh. Then he says to God, 'This is what you have made? He is empty. He is all hollow.'"

End of hadith. What should one take from this absurd tale? I know.

Humans are a long emptiness waiting to be filled.

Actually, Kaveh Akbar said it.

The bright side? It is left to you to figure it all out. You get to choose what fills you and therefore what defines you. It is only mildly surprising that mostly what people choose is self-centred and self-satisfying.

But it does one good to keep in mind that God also gave us nine holes. So no matter how much you fill yourself up, much of it is going to find its way out. Denying you fulfilment. satiation. So it's best to soak up as much as you can.

Faith, love, ambition, family, friends, kids, and death will serve you well. Maybe not death. It's no fun. Don't believe me, just ask the dead. Exactly.
