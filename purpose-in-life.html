What Is The Purpose Of Having A Purpose In Life?

Could it be true that Mahatma Gandhi was trying to unalive himself every time he organised a hunger strike? He picks an issue and makes demands that no self-respecting colonising power would agree to. Everything is carefully planned. Deliberate even. Then he waits, satiating his appetite with the sweetness of approaching death. He is reaching out his hand. People think he is yearning for food (they shake their heads in grief), but Gandhi doesn’t care. He can see the light at the end of the tunnel. He is there. And soon will become has been—

A cheer breaks out of the crowd. The British have conceded to his demands. Gandhi wants to fume, ‘such cowards,’ but he can’t. He is bereft of energy. So he smiles at his own fate. Everyone smiles with him. He’s given a roti to munch on, but the curry has no salt in it.

Why, he asks? Tax, they shout back. He takes in a deep breath. ‘On to the next thing,’ he says to himself. This time, they'd better not give in.

What? You never thought about this? Not even once. Okay, lets move on then.

Now that I have your attention lets dig in.

Purpose. Meaning. Direction… stability?

What is the point of life?

First of all, why is it a point? Why not a rectangle? A circle? Or a trapezium… that's the extent of my knowledge of shapes.

You know more? Good for you.

Before we even address the question, I think it's better to tackle the purposes humans assign themselves. Let's do it one by one.

AMBITION

To most, being ambitious is a very desirable quality. I personally have beef with ambition. I think human ambition had done more bad than it has done good.

Genghis Khan was ambitious. Some would argue his ambition is the most ambition has ever ambitioned. The result: 40 million killed. The only positive, all the decaying bodies probably added a lot of nutrition to the top soil.

In the present we have Dolund Trumpet. The most ambitious man of our times. This man sitting all the way across the world, doing what he pleases, has somehow made me prowl through the city to find an LPG gas cylinder.

If all the good human ambition had done adds up to the size of an asteroid, then the bad would have to be a star. My choice is Alpha Centauri.

How can something so irreversibly ugly be the purpose of our entire species?

FAITH

This greater being who is capable of no wrongdoing. Who, in spite of being so great and all encompassing, couldn’t come up with a better hobby than people watching?

You are bored out of your mind, scrolling reels, and apparently God is watching you watch the reels.

Faith requires obedience and acceptance, to shut down.

Most people find it appealing. For it is simple, vapid, easy.

The answer is always the same no matter the question. Which makes questions irrelevant.

But being curious is our species foundation.

LOVE

Ah, love. This magical, elusive thing, mostly unreachable.

If and when you do manage to grab it, it supposedly loses its shine.

Love also encompasses family and friends.

Love is selfless, so noble.

Love is selfless, so you give up your ways, maybe career, maybe freewill.

It still makes your partner selfish for making you do them.

It’s like someone pushes you into a sewer and then joins you. It stinks, but you have each other to close your noses.

REPRODUCTION

Kids. The result is kids.

To be fair, this is done by every species on planet earth.

And that's precisely why we can’t have the same purpose.

How embarrassing to have the same purpose in life as cockroaches, camels, or octopuses.

We, with our self-consciousness and intelligence, tend to call it legacy, continuation, meaning.

DEATH

Destination is grave. People who hurry in life tend to get there quicker.

Death removes unease, yes, but does removal equal comfort?

Does eliminating “un” from uncomfortable make one comfortable?

No.

It leaves us in a state with no name. To not be. To not feel.

How could not-being be a purpose?

Humans are a long emptiness waiting to be filled.

The bright side? It is left to you to figure it all out.

You get to choose what fills you and therefore what defines you.

Faith, love, ambition, family, friends, kids, and death will serve you well.

Maybe not death.

It’s no fun. Don’t believe me, just ask the dead. Exactly.
